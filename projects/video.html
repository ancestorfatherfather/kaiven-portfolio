Optics Principle Demo Video (AE Effects)

Group project (3-person team) · Adobe After Effects · Element 3D · Editing & compositing

1. Overview

A coursework science-demo video covering optics principles (a housing scene, pinhole imaging and apertures). The team worked as "script first, then segmented production, finally unified compositing": each member produced roughly one third of the film, and I assembled the final cut.

My roleLead creator of the middle segment (~1 minute) + editor of the full film
ToolsAdobe After Effects (Element 3D plugin, AE effects & animation)
ContentHousing scene, pinhole-imaging principle, aperture effects
Team contributionMerged all three segments into one film, unified pacing & transitions, added subtitles, synthesized the AI voice-over

2. Final film

3. My contributions

  • 3D animation: imported 3D objects with After Effects' Element 3D plugin, built the housing scene and produced the ~1-minute middle segment;
  • Visual effects: camera moves, lighting effects and transitions via AE built-ins to visualize pinhole imaging and aperture dynamics;
  • Editing: assembled all three members' segments into one coherent film with unified rhythm and transitions;
  • Subtitles & voice-over: added subtitles for the entire film and synthesized a consistent AI narration track.
This project took me through the full production pipeline — storyboard, segmented production, engineering-grade compositing — and trained my ability to manage schedule and quality across a small team.
